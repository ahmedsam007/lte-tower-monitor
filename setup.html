Set up Tower Monitor

Connect to your D-Link router once. After that, Tower Monitor opens straight to your dashboard.

Router password

The password you use to sign in to the router's web page. If you never changed it, it's printed on the sticker under the router.

Connecting to the router. This can take up to a minute…

    Switch SSH on yourself:

    1. Open the router's page with the button below, and sign in.
    2. Go to System → Telnet/SSH (on some routers: Management or Remote access).
    3. Tick SSH, leave the port at 22, and press Apply.
    4. Come back here and press Connect again.
    Open the router's page
    ✓

    All set

    Opening your dashboard…

    Your password is saved only on this computer. Tower Monitor talks to the router over your home network; nothing is sent to the internet.
